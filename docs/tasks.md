## Sequencing notes (recommended adjustments, pending user approval)

The tasks below stay in their spec milestone. The dependencies are listed explicitly. Recommended moves:

1. **Keycloak realm (W4-OPS-01) → Week 1, day 1.** W1-FE-03 (login) cannot be tested without it. (G1)
2. **Token validation and `require_role` (W4-BE-01), user sync (W4-BE-02), and the audit writer (W4-BE-03, writer part only) → start of Week 2.** The Week 2 endpoints (PATCH errors, PUT review items) and the Week 3 review decisions need the reviewer identity from the token and must write audit entries. Adding them afterwards means reworking every router. (G1)
3. **Service, team, and routing endpoints, backend only (W4-BE-06, W4-BE-07 routing part) → Week 2**, or seed rows with SQL for development. `severity_rules` and `build_report` (W2-BE-07, W2-BE-09) need criticality, teams, thresholds, and routing. (G2)
4. **Scheduler loop (W1-BE-13)**, `/api/dashboard/summary` (W3-BE-05), `/api/me` (W4-BE-01), retention (W5-BE-02), and retry (W3-BE-06) are in the spec but not in Section 16. They are placed where their consumers need them. (G44)
5. The frontend builds against `docs/api.md` with local mock data until the matching backend task lands. Backend Pydantic models must match api.md field names exactly.
6. Section 15 decisions #1, #4, and #5 must be answered before Week 1 starts. #9 is needed by the end of Week 1, and #2, #3, #6, and #7 by the start of Week 2. #8 is needed by the start of Week 3.
